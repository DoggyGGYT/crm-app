## 📋 What is crm-app?

crm-app is an open-source alternative to HubSpot. It helps you manage your contacts, companies, and deals in one place. The app is built for SaaS businesses looking for a lightweight, easy-to-use CRM tool. It uses OpenClaw technology for smooth handling of your sales data.

You do not need to be a tech expert to get started. The focus is on keeping your sales process organized and simple.

## 🖥️ System Requirements

Before installing, check that your Windows PC meets these requirements:

## 🔧 How to Use crm-app

crm-app is designed for simple and fast sales management.

### Contacts

- Add new contacts by clicking the "Add Contact" button.
- Fill out basic fields like name, email, phone, and company.
- Use the search bar to find contacts quickly.

### Companies

- Create companies to keep your contacts organized.
- Link contacts to companies to see the bigger picture.
- Track your interactions and notes with each company.

### Deals

- Add deals linked to contacts or companies.
- Update deal stages like "Lead," "Negotiation," or "Closed."
- Track deal value and expected close date.

### Dashboard

- The main dashboard shows your sales pipeline at a glance.
- Use filters to focus on specific contacts, companies, or deal stages.
- The interface updates in real time as you add or update data.

## ⚙️ Features Overview

- Open source, so anyone can review or contribute to the code.
- Built to be lightweight and fast.
- Uses SQLite to store your data locally.
- Pipeline views to manage sales stages visually.
- Easy import and export of contacts and deals.
- Supports multiple user accounts for teams.
- Runs entirely on Windows without extra software.

## 🔒 Data Privacy and Security

- Your data is stored on your computer.
- crm-app does not send your data to external servers.
- You control backup and export of your contacts and deals.
- All data files use simple SQLite databases for easy access.
